# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
The owner (an IB Diploma student) and their IB friends. They revise at home on laptops and phones, often against the clock in the weeks before exams, alone or comparing progress with friends.

## Product Purpose
EmberMath is a free question bank and exam trainer for IB Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches HL and Applications and Interpretation HL. It exists so students can practice in the real exam format (Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3 questions with full markschemes), at real exam timings, get marked instantly where the exam allows, and see exactly where their marks and minutes go. Success: students sit papers that feel like the real thing and know what to fix next.

## Positioning
Free, exam-faithful practice built only from original or openly licensed questions (no copyrighted IB papers or commercial banks), with every answer checked twice, plus a feedback loop on weak topics and pacing that generic banks do not give.

## Operating Context
Long revision sessions on a laptop, quick sessions on a phone. Timed papers, self-marking against markschemes, a reference panel (data/formula booklet, calculator, Desmos) beside the question, and a statistics page across sittings. Progress is stored per browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Keep every current feature: landing page, dock navigation with previews, hub, question bank, practice with timers and scratchpad, exam builder with real paper specs, statistics and feedback with history, sheets with simulations, reference panel, keyboard shortcuts, skeleton loading.
- Static site on Cloudflare Workers; not listed on search engines; American English.
- Target bank size: about 2000 questions per HL course; SL sees its subset.
- Content only from original writing or openly licensed sources, with attribution.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: EmberMath. User rules: no purple, violet, indigo, pink, rose, blush, magenta, salmon or peach (including tints); no decorative gradients; no all-caps tracked labels.

## Evidence on Hand
Real question bank, real 2025 paper specifications, live sites. No testimonials, users counts or results exist: do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Exam truth first: formats, timings and marking match the real papers.
- Bold landing, calm app: the landing earns attention; study screens stay quiet for long sessions.
- Every number is verified; never fabricate content or claims.
- Speed and focus over decoration.
